Choose the environment

In air, the feather is slowed by air resistance. In a vacuum, air resistance is removed.

Select Air or Vacuum, then press Drop Objects. Watch which object reaches the ground first.

What this shows

Gravity pulls both objects downward.
Air resistance can slow wide, light objects like a feather.
In a vacuum, both objects fall together because there is no air resistance.
Expected result
Air The metal ball lands first. The feather falls slowly because air pushes against it.
Vacuum The feather and metal ball land together. Gravity accelerates both equally.
