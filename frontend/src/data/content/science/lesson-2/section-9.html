Wien's Law and Stellar Temperatures

A star's color depends directly on its surface temperature. The cooler a star is, the longer the wavelength of light it radiates, causing it to glow red (around 3,000 K). Extremely hot stars radiate high-energy, short-wavelength light, glowing brilliant blue (above 20,000 K). Pure white stars sit balanced in the middle.
